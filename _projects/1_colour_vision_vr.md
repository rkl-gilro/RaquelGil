Virtual reality lets us change what cannot be changed in the real world: the illumination, the
objects around a target or the colour of its local surround, while observers stay immersed in a
realistic scene. We use calibrated head mounted displays and game engines (Unreal, Unity) to
study which cues human colour constancy relies on, and to compare human observers with
computational models.
